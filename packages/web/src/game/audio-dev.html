Questions for the listener

  1. Brake sawtooth vs boost sine — does brake actually read as "lower, rougher" than boost, or just buzzy/wrong? If wrong, what should replace it?
  2. Alarm pitch rise (500→900 Hz) — does rising pitch alongside rising gain read as urgency, or is it distracting/annoying at high intensity? Is the range right?
  3. Is the ambient drone (60 Hz / gain 0.04) audible at all on a phone speaker, or too quiet? Too loud on headphones?
  4. Does the goal chime (C5 E5 G5, 0.14 s stagger) read as a fanfare, or as three disconnected beeps?
  5. Any audible click or pop on boost/brake press/release, or on mute toggle?
  6. Overall mix — does anything clip or distort when boost + brake + alarm + a chime overlap?
